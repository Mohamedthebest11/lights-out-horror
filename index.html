Navigate rooms across a 4th dimension (W axis) from -1.00 to +1.00. Some closets and barriers phase in only when your 4D-W matches their phase.

Mobile: Left joystick to move, drag right screen to look, use W - / W + to shift 4D phase.
PC: WASD move, Mouse look, Z / X shift 4D phase, R/Click interact, F light, E pulse.

MOVE: WASD  |  LOOK: Mouse  |  4D PHASE: Z / X  |  OPEN / TAKE: R or Click  |  SPRINT: Shift  |  PULSE: E  |  LIGHT: F
